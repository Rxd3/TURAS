## Acoustic protocol

Use cached, hash-verified pinned Whisper-small and Turkish CV7 XLS-R parents.
First save actual unmodified-parent predictions/N-best for every validation ID
through the shared timing/export/validators, using three train clips for warm-up.
The initial one-pass run is baseline preservation, not the final three-repeat
RTF benchmark. No gold is passed to the adapters. D32 permits the predeclared
three-epoch budget after this succeeds; baseline metrics, checkpoint selection,
RDL fusion tuning and final test comparisons remain pending validation gold.

The unchanged CTC head cannot represent 14 characters in the shared-normalized
training targets: digits 0–9, `+`, `â`, `î`, `û`, affecting 3,232 references.
The strict profile remains available and refuses those targets. The separate
`ctc_xlsr_turkish_vocab_extension.json` experiment appends audited train-derived
IDs/rows, preserves the old rows and blank, and saves the extended processor.
It does not guess spoken numeral forms, change evaluation normalization, or drop
clips. Label results explicitly as fine-tuning **plus vocabulary extension**.

Local resolved configs, logs, progress and saved epochs are under
`artifacts/owner-m45/`. Training writes `progress.json` after the first optimizer
step and each ten further steps, plus per-epoch checksummed checkpoints. No
checkpoint is automatically called best. These files provide the execution
status; an approved protocol alone does not prove optimization has started.
