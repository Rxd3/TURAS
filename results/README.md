# Benchmark outputs and reports

No predictions, scores, benchmark tables or experiment results exist. Do not
fill report rows with zeros or invented measurements. Planned conditions are
listed in the [experiment protocol](../experiments/PROTOCOL.md).

M6 will curate small reviewed aggregate reports/run indexes here after receiving
genuine model bundles. Every result must link its frozen data split, matching
unmodified baseline, resolved configuration, evaluator/normalizer version,
edit/support counts, timing/hardware and artifact/run hashes. Include negative
findings, failures, unsupported capabilities and resource blocks.

Full prediction/N-best dumps, checkpoints and graph snapshots are external hashed
artifacts. Local `results/predictions/` and `results/nbest/` are ignored. Do not
create a mock leaderboard or imply an unrun baseline is complete.
